# Tiny Runner: Couch to 5K

A free Apple Watch app that coaches you from walking to running 5K with a friendly little runner, guided run/walk intervals and wrist taps.

## Get help

Found a bug or have a question? [Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/floBKK/tinyrunner/issues/new) and I'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**The app doesn't show my heart rate or distance.**
On your iPhone, open **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Tiny Runner** and turn on the permissions. For distance, also check **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Tiny Runner**.

**Do I need my iPhone with me?**
No. Tiny Runner runs entirely on your Apple Watch. Distance is most accurate on watches with GPS.

**Where are my runs saved?**
Each run is saved as a workout in Apple Health, so it appears in the Fitness app and counts toward your Activity rings.

**Is Tiny Runner free?**
Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts.

## Privacy

Tiny Runner doesn't collect any data. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.html).
