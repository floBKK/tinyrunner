# Privacy Policy

*Tiny Runner: Couch to 5K — effective September 30, 2026*

**Short version: Tiny Runner doesn't collect, store or share any of your data.**

## What the app uses

With your permission, Tiny Runner uses the following on your Apple Watch, only while you run:

- **Heart rate** from Apple Health, to show it on screen during your run.
- **Location (GPS)**, to measure how far you run.
- **Workouts** saved to Apple Health, so your runs appear in the Fitness app and count toward your Activity rings.

This information stays on your devices and in Apple Health. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app doesn't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No ads
- No internet connection is used by the app

## Your choices

You can turn off Health or Location access at any time in the iPhone **Settings** app (**Privacy & Security → Health** or **Location Services**). The app keeps working, without heart rate or distance.

## Children

Tiny Runner doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
